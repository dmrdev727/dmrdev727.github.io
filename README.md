## Design Decisions
1. Fundamentally, this launcher reflects how I like to setup my phone with configuration options available to setup the launcher in several other ways. My preferences are:
+ One row of icons and folders at the bottom of the home screen for easy access one handed.
+ App drawer displayed in fixed grid pages so I can built muscle memory to open apps quickly.
2. I have built in functionality that allows me to use my phone quicker:
+ I want the ability to add a specific URL as a Web Shortcut, not just a generic link to the website, a specific url.
+ A local search with access to app shortcuts and app activities. With the option to launch a web search in my default browser using my default search engine!
3. To ensure the app uses the least memory and processing possible, App icons are loaded in lazily. Meaning they are only loaded into memory when they are shown on screen which can result in a slight hitch on the first load of a App Drawer page etc. The benefit is that the app does not waste processing time or memory on icons you have not seen. The app then removes the icons from memory if the android system asks for memory space to be cleared.
4. To ensure the app is accessible, I have tried to ensure the whole app is navigable by controller, keyboard inputs, or screen reader inputs. Let me know if there is anything I can improve.

## Features


Find what you need! No need to dig through endless folders and nested menus to find that one specific setting or app. DMR Launcher Search puts everything you need right at your fingertips. Whether you’re looking for a specific app, trying to toggle a system setting, launching a custom shortcut, or deep-diving into a specific app activity—all results appear instantly. And if the answer isn't on your phone, you can jump straight to a web search in your default browser using your default search engine.<br/>

Declutter your home screen! Tired of a cluttered home screen filled with icons you rarely use? Our launcher offers a minimalist experience that puts your focus back on what matters. At the bottom of your screen, you’ll find a customisable Dock which is the perfect home for your most-used apps. You can even add folders to the dock to group related tools together, keeping everything organised and just one tap away. Whether you prefer our sleek default single-row layout or want to expand it to multiple rows, you have full control over how your workspace looks and functions.<br/>

Organise your apps! Overwhelmed by your apps? DMR Launcher gives you total control over how you browse your App Drawer with three distinct layout options: List, Horizontal Pages, and Vertical Pages. You can organise your apps into custom categories that appear as tabs along the top, allowing you to swipe quickly between groups for instant access. To ensure your workspace feels exactly right, we offer highly adjustable icon density and label sizing to suit your personal style.<br/>

Add Custom Web Shortcuts! No need to clutter your device with dozens of apps when a website performs just as well. With Website Shortcuts, you can add specific URLs directly into your App Drawer, bypassing common Web App limitations. This allows you to launch deep links or specific online tools instantly while keeping your phone lean—saving valuable storage space and preserving battery life by eliminating "app bloat." Get the functionality you need without the overhead of unnecessary installations.<br/>

Tailor it to your taste! Your launcher should be an extension of your style. With DMR Launcher, every component—from buttons and menus to icons—automatically updates to match your chosen theme settings for a seamless look. You have the power to pick custom accent colours via hex codes, adjust icon sizes and saturation levels, and choose between different background layouts. It’s not just about changing a colour; it’s about crafting an interface that feels exactly how you want it to.<br/>


Shortcut tedious apps! Don't just open an app, open a specific action. DMR Launcher supports pinned shortcuts that let you jump straight into deep functions or Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) from your browser. This allows you to create "fast-tracks" for frequent tasks: pin key contacts, open a Spotify playlist with one tap, or launch a dedicated web tool as if it were an installed app. By bypassing unnecessary home screens and menus, you can streamline your routine and keep your most-used functions front and center.<br/>


Stop the scroll! Fight back with our innovative Visual Deterrent system. DMR Launcher provides a subtle visual cue when you repeatedly open the same app within a 10-minute window by desaturating its icon. These "Repeated Use Visual Queues" are designed to break app addiction and help you stay mindful of how much time you spend in specific apps. By turning habit-tracking into a visual experience, the launcher helps you identify and break the cycles of doom-scrolling so you can stay productive and present.<br/>

Create a curated experience! Not every app needs to be front and center, but you still need access to them when you do need them. With our Hide Apps feature, you can remove unwanted applications from your App Drawer while ensuring they remain fully available in your search results or as shortcuts on your Dock. It’s the perfect way to create a clean, intentional app drawer while maintaining full functionality across your entire device.<br/>

Stay updated at a glance! Never miss an important notification with customisable visual indicators. Choose between a classic dot or a sleek coloured outline to highlight pending alerts on your icons. To ensure you never miss a beat, both options can pulse in and out to catch your eye when something new arrives. Completely optional and fully customisable, these indicators give you the perfect balance of staying informed without cluttering your screen.<br/>

Preserve your screen! Protect the longevity of your device with our built-in OLED burn-in protection. If your home screen remains active for an extended period, DMR Launcher automatically shifts the position of elements like the dock and widgets slightly every 15 seconds. This subtle, automatic movement prevents static pixels from causing permanent "burn-in," ensuring your display remains vibrant and clear for years to come.<br/>

Designed for everyone! We believe that great technology should be accessible to all. DMR Launcher is fully optimised for navigation via keyboard, gamepads, and screen readers. Whether you prefer using physical inputs or require assistive technologies, the entire interface provides a seamless and responsive experience. By removing barriers to interaction, we ensure that every user can enjoy a high-quality launcher that works perfectly with their chosen method of input.<br/>

Speed without compromise! Experience a launcher that prioritises your time and your device's performance. DMR Launcher provides a highly responsive interface where you can customize the animations to suit your taste or turn them off entirely for an ultra-fast, no-nonsense experience. It’s simple, it’s fast, and it’s designed to work perfectly with your lifestyle.<br/>
